Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short

  • YesterMe has no accounts. The game never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress, scores and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before ads are personalised. On iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt decides whether your advertising ID is shared.
  • We use Google Firebase to see how the game is played and to receive crash reports. Analytics does not use your advertising ID.
  • The "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • The game does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

YesterMe is a game for iPhone and Android made by an independent developer in Türkiye. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean that developer, who decides how the personal data described here is used (the "data controller"). This policy covers the YesterMe app and this website.

For any question or request about your data, write to yesterme.game@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The game keeps these in its private storage on your phone: the game in progress (so that you can continue later), your level and scores, your high score, your settings (sound, vibration, language and theme), whether you finished the tutorial, whether you bought "Remove Ads", and the dates it needs for its ad limits, such as the day you first played.

We don't receive this data. It is deleted when you delete the app. If your phone backs up app data, for example to iCloud or Google Drive, Apple or Google keeps that backup under their own terms.

3. Ads

The game shows a banner at the bottom of the game screen, a short ad between some games, and, only if you choose it, a video ad that rewinds your last five moves. The ads come from Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC and, for people in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, by Google Ireland Limited.

To request and show ads, the Google software built into the game collects:

  • your phone's advertising ID, the app set ID and other device identifiers, and, where applicable, identifiers of accounts signed in on the phone (on iPhone, the advertising ID only if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address, which is used to estimate your approximate location, such as your country or city;
  • information about your phone and the app, such as the model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you use the app and its ads, for example app launches, taps, ad views and whether a video was watched to the end, and diagnostic data such as how fast the app starts and how much energy it uses;
  • your consent choices.

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, only where you allow it, to personalise ads. Google uses this data as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Google may work with other advertising companies; where the consent form is shown, it lists them.

Your choices:

  • EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before the first ad is loaded, the game shows Google's consent form. You can agree or refuse, and change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Choices. If you refuse, you only see ads that are not personalised, or "limited" ads.
  • iPhone: before the first ad is loaded, the game asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising ID is not shared. You can change this in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings, usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Video ads are optional: one only plays when you tap its offer.
  • Remove Ads: after you buy it, the game no longer requests ads.

4. Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase, provided by Google, to understand how the game is played and to fix problems:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase records events in the game: the first launch, tutorial steps, the start and end of each game (level, score, moves, how it ended and how long it took), ad offers and whether they were watched, taps on "Invite", and purchase attempts and purchases (product and price). With them it collects a random ID for your copy of the app, your phone model, operating system, app version and language, and your approximate location (country or region) estimated from your IP address. We have turned off the collection of your advertising ID in Analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report when the app crashes: what the app was doing, the phone model and operating system, and a random installation ID. With it, Firebase also records basic session information (when you open the app and for how long you use it, and the phone model) to measure how stable the app is.
  • Firebase Remote Config lets us change some settings of the game without an update, such as how often short ads appear. For this, the app sends a random installation ID, your country, language and time zone, the versions of the app and the operating system, and when the app was first opened.

These IDs are random. They are not your name, your phone number or an account, and we don't try to find out who you are from them. We use this data only to improve the game: to balance its difficulty, to set how often ads appear, to see which features are used and to fix crashes. Google processes it on our behalf, as our service provider.

In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics and crash reports use their random IDs only if you agree in the consent form. You can change your mind in Settings → Privacy Choices.

5. The "Remove Ads" purchase

You buy "Remove Ads" through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment under their own terms and privacy policies, and we never receive your card details, name or address. The store tells the app which product was bought, with a transaction identifier and its status, so that the game can unlock it and later restore it (Settings → Restore Purchases).

6. Inviting friends

When you tap "Invite", your phone's share menu opens with a short text. You choose where it goes; we don't see whom you send it to.

7. When you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to reply to you. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail). We delete these emails within 12 months after the conversation ends, unless we need them longer for a legal claim.

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical data to deliver the pages and keep the service secure; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The site uses no cookies and no analytics, and it loads no fonts or scripts from other sites.

9. Why we may use your data

Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and Türkiye's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK), we rely on:

  • your consent for personalised ads, for tracking on iPhone and, in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, for analytics and crash reports with random IDs (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1));
  • our legitimate interests in funding a free game with ads that are not personalised, in measuring ads and preventing fraud, in analytics and crash reports where consent is not required, and in answering your emails (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f));
  • a contract with you, to deliver and restore the "Remove Ads" purchase (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c));
  • legal obligations, where a law requires us to keep or disclose data (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).

You can withdraw your consent at any time, in Settings → Privacy Choices or in your phone's settings. This does not affect what happened before.

The data is collected automatically, by electronic means, through the app and the Google software in it, and directly from you when you write to us.

10. Who receives data

  • Google, for ads (AdMob), for analytics, crash reports and remote settings (Firebase) and for email (Gmail), as described above;
  • Apple and Google, as the app stores, for downloads, purchases and refunds, under their own privacy policies;
  • GitHub, which hosts this website;
  • public authorities, if the law requires it.

These companies are bound by their own terms and data protection commitments, which protect the data at least as well as this policy describes. We do not sell personal data, and we do not give it to data brokers.

11. Data outside your country

Google, Apple and GitHub process data in the United States and in other countries. For data from the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, they rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions, or on standard contractual clauses. Transfers of data from Türkiye are made under Article 9 of the KVKK.

12. How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete the app or use Settings → Delete My Data.
  • Analytics: event data linked to a random ID is deleted after 2 months. After that, only overall statistics remain, which do not identify anyone.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • The random installation ID used by Firebase: until it is deleted, for example when you use Delete My Data.
  • Ad data: Google keeps it as its privacy policy describes.
  • Emails: up to 12 months after the conversation ends.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Article 11 of the KVKK or the privacy laws of some US states), you can:

  • ask whether we process your data, why, and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct it, to delete it or to limit how we use it, and to tell those we passed it to;
  • learn to whom your data was passed, in your country or abroad;
  • object to uses based on our legitimate interests, and opt out of targeted advertising;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • object to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis (we make no such decisions);
  • ask for compensation if unlawful use of your data causes you damage;
  • complain to a data protection authority: in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority, in the EEA the authority of your country, in the United Kingdom the Information Commissioner's Office.

The quickest way is in the app: Settings → Delete My Data erases what the game stores on your phone and resets the random IDs used by analytics, and Settings → Privacy Choices changes your consent. Data already sent is then no longer linked to your new IDs and is deleted after the periods in section 12; ad data is held by Google under its own policy. For anything else, write to yesterme.game@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, we may not be able to find data about you unless you tell us more, such as your phone model and when you played. We reply within 30 days, free of charge.

14. Children

YesterMe is made for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has sent us personal data, for example by email, write to us and we will delete it.

15. Security

We don't run our own servers or databases for the game. The data the app sends to Google is encrypted in transit. The data on your phone stays in the app's private storage. Our developer and email accounts are protected with two-step verification.

16. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, for example a new kind of data, we will tell you in the game before it takes effect.
